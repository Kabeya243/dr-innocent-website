About

Meet Dr Innocent Ayesiga

A Ugandan medical doctor, global health and public health practitioner, and advocate.

Dr Innocent Ayesiga is a Ugandan medical doctor, global health and public health practitioner, and advocate with interests in health systems research and strengthening, infectious diseases, antimicrobial resistance, maternal and child health, artificial intelligence in healthcare, mental health, and tropical medicine.

He holds a Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBChB) and a Master of Public Health (2024–2026), both from Mbarara University of Science and Technology, with additional training in project management, a postgraduate diploma in clinical trials from the University of Montpellier in France, and expertise in implementation science from both the University of Washington and the World Health Organization. He is also a British Academy writing scholar from the British Institute in Eastern Africa, Nairobi.

His work emphasises translating research findings into practical interventions — work that has been used to develop national clinical guidelines and featured at the recently completed 79th World Health Assembly in Geneva.

“Evidence generation to strengthen health systems and improve healthcare delivery — particularly across sub-Saharan Africa.”

From evidence to policy

Part of his research has been used to develop national clinical guidelines, moving findings directly into frontline care — and his work was featured at the 79th World Health Assembly in Geneva. His scholarly themes connect antimicrobial resistance, mental health, mpox, infectious diseases of poverty, non-communicable diseases, health systems and global health partnerships.
